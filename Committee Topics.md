# Background Committees
## UNESCO - Linguistic and Cultural Preservation in a Globalized World
### **Description:**

The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) promotes international cooperation through the preservation of shared heritage and cultural diversity within education. With an estimated 7,100 languages spoken across the world and around 40% classified as endangered, linguistic diversity faces an unprecedented level of erosion. In this committee, delegates will discuss the causes and consequences of linguistic and cultural erasure, ranging from colonialism to modern technology and globalization. Delegates are expected to develop strategies to safeguard and revitalize endangered languages and cultures.



## UNEP - Energy Discourse in Eurasia
### **Description:**

The General Assembly committee on Energy Discourse in Eurasia focuses on how energy resources influence political power, economic stability and regional security. Energy, despite being a vital aspect of a country’s function, can become an area of discourse and geopolitical leverage. In this committee, delegates will consider how participating nations can ensure global access to reliable energy sources while addressing and preventing instabilities that arise as a result. Delegates are expected to develop practical solutions to global energy issues that use well-rounded and multifaceted approaches, coming to collaborative resolutions.

# Specialized Committees

## We Will Rebuild This Cathedral Together - The Burning of Notre Dame 2019
### **Description:**

Despite being called a “predictable surprise,” the burning of the Notre-Dame Cathedral of Paris in 2019 sent global shockwaves as one of France’s most significant buildings was set ablaze. After heroic efforts were made to stop the fire, an important question remained: What happens next? Delegates will represent various officials to discuss how to rebuild the renowned landmark, while considering social, cultural, religious, and economic stakes. Will the court of public opinion agree to the plan of action? Only time shall tell.




## SCOTUS - Youngstown Sheet $ Tube Co V. Sawyer
### **Description:**

The Supreme Court of the United States stands as an integral intersection between law, politics, and executive power. During the height of the Korean War, 1952, steelworkers believed that they were being undervalued. A desire for higher wages and a mixture of complications with working environments forced workers to go on a Steelworkers Strike. President Truman believed that for the betterment of the country, a seizure of steel mills was needed. In this committee, delegates will be challenged to cooperate and collaborate to decide the ruling of President Truman’s actions. Will you help the workers or agree with the President’s actions?


## WHO - Addressing the Escalation of Antimicrobial Resistance in South Asia
### **Description:**

Over decades, factors such as over-the-counter antibiotic use, high population density, limited access to healthcare, and environmental contamination have merged to accelerate the emergence of drug-resistant infections. Recently, AMR cases have suddenly peaked within the Southern region of Asia. This problem will increasingly evolve throughout the committee until it grows into an urgent public health crisis. During the conference, delegates must balance regional realities with global responsibilities to develop coordinated strategies to stop the spread to neighboring countries, effectively saving both South Asia and the world.



## Cool Britannia - The Battle of Britpop
### **Description:**

The year is 1995. A new musical movement is in full swing in the UK: Britpop. In this specialised committee, delegates must vie for the coveted No.1 chart position. Britain is changing: music, media, and politics are all colliding. As Cool Britannia emerges and social tensions simmer, diplomacy, strategy, and cultural influence will prove to be just as important as record sales. In this specialised committee, delegates must vie for the coveted No.1 chart position. Oasis and Blur are releasing singles on the same day, and only one can emerge victorious. Britain is changing: music, media, and politics are all colliding. Can you dominate the charts without tearing Britpop – and Britain – apart?

## Percy Jackson - Old Gods in Modern Times
### **Description:**

With less and less people believing in the religions of ancient civilizations, the Olympians and major gods of Greek mythology have grown weaker. At the same time, the balance of power has shifted, as already neglected minor gods grow more daring in opposing the Olympians. In this committee, delegates will take the role of a Greek demi-god. Will you be able to adapt the cultures of ancient Greece to fit into modern society? Or will these ancient deities disappear, only to be found in the history books? Their fate is in your hands.

## An Era of Conflict  - The Unification of Japan
### **Description:**

The unification of Japan sought to end centuries of conflict and chaos that defined the region for years. This period in Japanese history is known as the Sengoku period, a time of constant usurpation, and when nobody could become a ruler. This committee will take place midway through the Sengoku period after 1560, shortly after Oda Nobunaga secured the Owari province and established himself as one of the prominent Daimyō of the age. However, much like the other Daimyō, his ambitions do not stop at controlling just a portion of Japan. Delegates will navigate through the harsh political landscape of feudal Japan, utilizing their given powers and resources to decide the fate of Japan and end the centuries-long bloodshed.
