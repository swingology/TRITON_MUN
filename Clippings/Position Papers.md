Position papers for TritonMUN XXXI are due **April 3rd at 11:59pm**.

Extensions for position papers must be sent by the advisor to TritonMUN by **March 22nd**.

**Minimum length for position papers are 1 page, with the maximum length being 2 pages.**

Formatting for position papers include:

- Double-spaced in Times New Roman 12 pt. font
- No pictures
- At the top of each paper, the delegate’s country/person, name, committee, and topic
- Required\* sections clearly labeled

\*Some committees may ask for different formatting or an alternative to a position paper altogether, this will be clearly noted in the background guide for that committee

**Position papers must be submitted to the Google Forms linked within the respective background guide.**
